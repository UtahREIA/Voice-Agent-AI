# Finding help beyond our partners

Lani only matches from Utah REIA's partner list, and partners pay us a referral fee. There are good providers in Utah who are not partners. Here is where to start on your own.

## Check a license (free official lookups)

- **Real estate agents, brokers and appraisers:** Utah Division of Real Estate license search. https://secure.utah.gov/rer/relv/search.html
- **Mortgage brokers and loan officers:** NMLS Consumer Access. https://www.nmlsconsumeraccess.org
- **Contractors, trades, CPAs and home inspectors:** Utah Division of Professional Licensing (DOPL) license lookup. https://verify.commerce.utah.gov/verify/Search.aspx?facility=Y (home inspectors are newly licensed in Utah as of January 1, 2026)
- **Attorneys:** Utah State Bar, Find a Lawyer. https://www.utahbar.org/public-services/find-a-lawyer/
- **Insurance agents and title agents:** Utah Insurance Department licensee search. https://insurance.utah.gov/licensee-search
- **Investment advisers and brokers:** Investor.gov, Check Out Your Investment Professional. https://www.investor.gov

## Help with no state license

Private and hard money lenders on investment loans, wholesalers, bookkeepers, coaches, mentors and courses usually have no license to check. Ask for references from investors who have used them.

## Before you hire anyone

- Compare a few before you choose.
- Ask other investors who they use. Utah REIA meetups are a good place to ask.
- Read everything before you sign, and get your own advice before you send money.
- Be careful with anyone who guarantees returns or pushes you to decide fast.
